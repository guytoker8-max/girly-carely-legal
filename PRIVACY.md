# Privacy Policy — Girly Carely

**Last updated: 7 August 2026**

## The short version

Girly Carely does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and
no network connection. Everything you log stays on your phone.

## What the app stores

The app stores what you enter, on your device only:

- the pill name and reminder time you set
- which days you marked a pill as taken, and at what time
- period start dates, cycle length and period length
- water logged per day, and your daily goal
- daily mood, symptoms, little wins, and your one-line notes
- your settings (quiet hours, reminder cadence, saved bottle sizes)

This is written to your device's local app storage. It is not encrypted at rest beyond
whatever encryption your phone itself applies, and it is not transmitted anywhere.

## What the app does not do

- **No account.** You never create one, and there is nothing to sign in to.
- **No servers.** The app makes no network requests. There is no backend to send data to.
- **No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.**
- **No tracking.** Nothing about you is profiled, shared, sold, or used for advertising.
- **No third-party data sharing.** There is no third party involved.

## When data leaves your phone

Only when you explicitly choose to send it, using two features:

- **Share as PDF** builds a summary on your device and hands it to your phone's own share
  sheet, so you can send it to a doctor.
- **Back up your data** writes a plain (unencrypted) JSON file and hands it to the same
  share sheet.

In both cases the app's involvement ends there. Where the file goes — email, cloud drive,
messaging app — is determined by what you pick in your phone's share sheet, and is then
covered by that service's own privacy policy, not this one. Because backups are plain JSON,
treat the file as sensitive: anyone who obtains it can read your logged health data.

## Permissions

- **Notifications / alarms** are used solely to show your pill reminder and water nudges on
  your own device. No notification content is sent anywhere.
- **Files** access appears only when you pick a backup file to restore.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of it. There is no server copy, so there is nothing to request
deletion of and nothing for us to delete on your behalf. Any backup files you exported
yourself remain wherever you saved them, and only you can remove those.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.

## Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

Rights such as access, portability, correction and erasure exist against whoever holds your
personal data. Because your data never leaves your device, the developer of this app never
receives, stores, or processes it, and therefore holds nothing to disclose or delete. You
exercise these rights directly: the data is in your possession, the export feature gives you
portability, and uninstalling gives you erasure.

We do not sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined by the CCPA.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it. Material changes will
be noted in the app's release notes.
